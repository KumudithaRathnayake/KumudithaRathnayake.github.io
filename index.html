EXPERTISE

Materials Characterization

AUTOMATED SPECTRAL ANALYSIS

From raw spectra to structural answers

I build Python workflows that take candidate structures, simulate their X-ray spectra (FDMNES, Quanty) and compare them with experimental data automatically. I am also developing a machine-learning approach (pyFitIt) to identify active-state structures from operando XAS.
